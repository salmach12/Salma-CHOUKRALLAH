About me

Morocco → China Student → Developer Learning → Exploring → Creating

I'm a Moroccan international student who moved to China to study Software Engineering, and somewhere along the way university stopped being just about programming. It became about figuring out a new country too — how to order food without knowing the language yet, how to read a metro map in characters I couldn't pronounce, how to make a room feel like home.

These past three years have also been about learning languages, meeting people from everywhere, traveling when I could afford to, reading more than I probably should when I have deadlines, taking photos of ordinary moments, and slowly figuring out what I actually want to build once all of this coursework turns into something real.

This site is my attempt to put all of that in one place — not a résumé, just the actual pieces.
